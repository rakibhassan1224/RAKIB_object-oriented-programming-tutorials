2.1 Why are the variables declared as private?
To enforce encapsulation and prevent direct unauthorized modification of sensitive object state from outside the class.

2.2 What happens if the variables are declared public?
Any external class can modify the field values directly without validation, breaking data integrity and control.

2.3 Why do we use getters and setters?
To provide controlled read and write access to private fields, allowing validation and maintaining internal implementation flexibility.
